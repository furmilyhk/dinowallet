# Support

**DinoWallet 恐龍記帳**

Something wrong, or something missing? Open an issue:
<https://github.com/furmilyhk/dinowallet/issues>

## Common questions

**Does it connect to my bank?** No. Nothing is linked and no login is asked
for. You add what you spend, or photograph the receipt.

**Where is my data?** On your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if that is on.
Turn on **Local only** in Settings to keep it to one device.

**Scanning gives me an empty form.** Reading a receipt uses Apple's on-device
model where the device has it. Without it, the app falls back to rules that
find the shop and the total but leave the category to you.

**I paid and the app still says free.** Open Membership and tap **Restore
purchases**. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you bought
with.

**How do I cancel?** iOS Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions. Deleting the
app does not cancel a subscription.

**Can my family see the same book?** Yes — Settings ▸ Family ▸ Share with
family. Everyone invited sees the same entries and budgets.
